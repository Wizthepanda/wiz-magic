## 🎯 What Makes This Special?

### **1. Auto-Selection**
No need to manually select videos one by one. We auto-select all your videos after connection.

### **2. YouTube Channel Handle**
Your videos will display with your actual YouTube username (e.g., **@facelessavatars7049**) instead of your Google account name.

### **3. Per-Video Categories**
Each video gets its own category and subcategory, ensuring accurate discovery filtering.

### **4. Bulk Assignment**
Use "Apply to All Videos" to quickly assign the same category to multiple videos at once.

## ❓ FAQ

### **Q: Can I edit categories after publishing?**
Yes! You can update video categories anytime from the "Published" tab.

### **Q: Why does my username show @handle instead of my name?**
We use your YouTube channel handle to maintain consistency with your YouTube brand.

### **Q: What if I don't have a YouTube channel handle?**
We'll use your channel title as a fallback (e.g., "Faceless Avatars").

### **Q: Can I unpublish videos?**
Yes! Go to the "Published" tab and click "Unpublish" on any video.

### **Q: How many videos can I publish at once?**
We fetch your 20 most recent videos. You can select all or just a few.

### **Q: Do I need to reconnect every time?**
No! Once connected, your YouTube channel stays connected until you disconnect.

## 🎉 Tips for Success

### **1. Choose Accurate Categories**
Help users discover your content by selecting the most relevant category and subcategory.

### **2. Publish Regularly**
Keep your content fresh by publishing new videos as you create them on YouTube.

### **3. Use Bulk Assignment**
If all your videos are in the same niche, use "Apply to All Videos" to save time.

### **4. Check Your Username**
Make sure your YouTube channel has a custom URL/handle for the best branding.
